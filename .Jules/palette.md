## 2026-04-16 - Accessible Layout Controls
**Learning:** Icon-only layout controls (like activity bar or window panels) require both `title` and `aria-label` to ensure they are discoverable for both visual hover and screen reader users, especially conditionally rendered states.
**Action:** Add dual `title` and `aria-label` attributes to all newly introduced layout controls to maintain accessibility.
