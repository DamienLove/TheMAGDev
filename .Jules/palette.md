## 2024-03-16 - Studio & Paywall Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons without `aria-label` or `title` attributes are completely opaque to screen readers, making critical UI functions inaccessible.
**Action:** Consistently ensure that all buttons utilizing only visual icons (like Material Symbols) include an explicit `aria-label` that describes the button's action.
