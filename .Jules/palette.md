## 2026-04-13 - Activity Bar and Panel Accessibility
**Learning:** All icon-only buttons in the workspace layout (like activity bar components and bottom panel controls in CodeEditor.tsx) must utilize explicit aria-label and title attributes to ensure screen reader accessibility compliance and hover discoverability.
**Action:** Always add aria-label and title attributes to icon-only buttons in workspace layouts.
