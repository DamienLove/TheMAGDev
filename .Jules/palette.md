## 2024-03-09 - Sidebar Icon-Only Buttons Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons across the app layout (especially in the sidebar, code editor, and app layout) were frequently lacking both `aria-label` and `title` attributes, making them inaccessible to screen readers and difficult to understand without hovering on certain desktop screen sizes.
**Action:** When creating new icon-only buttons or reviewing existing ones, ensure both `aria-label` and `title` are consistently applied.
