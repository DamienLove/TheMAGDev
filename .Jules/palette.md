## 2024-03-09 - Projects View Accessibility
**Learning:** Search inputs relying solely on placeholders, and filter button groups lacking `aria-pressed` state, degrade the screen reader experience in listing views.
**Action:** Always add explicit `aria-label` to search inputs and `aria-pressed` to active filter buttons to ensure context is conveyed to assistive technologies.
