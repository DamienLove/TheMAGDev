## 2026-05-10 - Missing ARIA labels in Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Many icon-only buttons in the workspace layout lack 'aria-label' and 'title' attributes, causing accessibility and hover discoverability issues.
**Action:** Always include explicit 'aria-label' and 'title' attributes when creating or modifying icon-only buttons in UI components.
