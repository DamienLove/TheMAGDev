## 2025-04-07 - Screen Reader Compliance for Editor Toolbars
**Learning:** Icon-only action buttons in the workspace layout (like activity bar actions and bottom panel controls) are frequently missed for explicit tooltips and accessible names, impacting screen reader usability.
**Action:** Always add both `title` and `aria-label` to icon-only controls in IDE layouts to ensure proper screen reader compliance and mouse hover discoverability.
